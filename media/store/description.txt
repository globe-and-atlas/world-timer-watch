Two cities. One clear view of the day.

World Timer combines large city clocks, a schematic world map and aligned next-24-hour call-window strips. Choose Main and Other from 40 named cities, each with its own time zone and daylight-saving rules.

Amber-on-black is the default. Choose Olive LCD or custom background, text and accent colors. Double-knock the watch case to send a dot travelling between your cities; the clocks stay readable. Travel can be disabled.

Set daily call hours for each city, including overnight windows. These are your configured hours, not live availability detection. Choose 12/24-hour display, optional seconds and date formats including Off, ISO, US and European.

Native layouts for Pebble Time 2, Time Round and Round 2.

After updating, open settings to choose your cities and call hours. Previous manual UTC offsets are replaced by explicit city choices. Offline time-zone data uses IANA 2026f rules covering 2020 through 2037; future rule changes require an update.

The native animated preview demonstrates travel followed by separately selected color settings. Colors do not cycle automatically on the watch.
